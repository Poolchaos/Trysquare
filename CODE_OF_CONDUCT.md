# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise work, not the person who wrote it. Assume the
other person is trying to get it right.

## What is expected

This project is deliberately blunt about code. Reviews here are direct, and a
change can be rejected for reasons that are stated plainly. That directness
applies to work, never to people.

Concretely:

- Critique the code, the design, or the claim. Not the author's competence,
  background, or motives.
- Say what is wrong and why, with evidence. "This is broken" without a reason
  is not a review, it is noise.
- Accept that being wrong is normal and correcting quickly is the point. That
  applies to the maintainer as much as to anyone else.
- Disagreement is welcome and useful. Contempt is not.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Discrimination or prejudicial remarks of any kind, including about age,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, personal
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or persistently pushing past a clear no.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits, and any other channel where you are participating in the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer privately via a GitHub direct message or by
opening a [security advisory](https://github.com/Poolchaos/Trysquare/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Depending on severity, the response may be
a private word, a public correction, removal of the offending content, or a
block from the project. The maintainer decides, and will explain the reasoning
where it is reasonable to do so.

This project does not have a large governance structure. It has one maintainer
and an expectation that people behave like adults.
